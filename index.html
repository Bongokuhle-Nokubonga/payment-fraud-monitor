    The rules

    Each rule that fires adds points. 60+ is high risk, 30-59 needs review, below 30 is low. One weak signal on its own never blocks a card.

    Card testing 30

    3+ charges under R20 within 10 minutes: checking a stolen card works.

    Large purchase after test charges 35

    R1 000+ within an hour of a card-testing burst.

    Impossible travel 40

    In-person payments in two cities faster than 900 km/h. Usually a cloned card.

    Approved after repeated declines 30

    3+ declines in 30 minutes, then success: CVV or expiry guessing.

    Much bigger than usual 30

    5× the card's typical (median) spend, and at least R2 000.

    Unusually many transactions 20

    5 or more on one card within an hour.

    First use in a new country 20

    A country this card has never been used in before.

    High-risk merchant type 15

    Gambling, crypto, gift cards and money transfer: easy places to cash out.

    High value at night 10

    R5 000+ between midnight and 05:00.
